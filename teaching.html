Firms in Macro-Finance: Tools and Applications

BSE Summer School, 2023 · Syllabus

How firm-level frictions and shocks shape firm decisions and industry and aggregate outcomes: analytical tools to solve firm dynamics models with frictions and misallocation, and empirical tools to test their predictions, with a special emphasis on financial frictions.

Finance, Firm Dynamics, and Aggregate Outcomes

Short course, December 2020 · Syllabus

Empirical methods to identify firm-level frictions, a basic heterogeneous-firm dynamics framework and its extensions with frictions, and applications to the aggregate implications of financial frictions.
